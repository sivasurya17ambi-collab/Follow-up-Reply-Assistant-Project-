Problem Statement

The Business Communication Challenge

Manual Overhead

Sales reps and engineering leads spend 20%+ of their day deciphering meeting notes and hand-crafting follow-up emails.

Dropped Action Items

Critical commitments (deliverable dates, security packs, quote revisions) are frequently forgotten or lost in transition.

Tone Misalignment

Difficulty adapting language between formal C-suite executive summaries and persuasive sales re-engagement.

Lack of Audit & Citations

Raw LLMs hallucinate context without grounding or transparent source citations back to the meeting transcript.

System Design

Three-Tier Architecture

Decoupled Full-Stack Architecture

  • Frontend (Streamlit): Executive multi-tab interface with live interactive draft editor and one-click quick loads.
  • Backend API (FastAPI): High-performance async REST endpoints with Pydantic V2 data validation and Swagger documentation.
  • RAG Intelligence (Chroma & LangChain): Hierarchical text chunker (400 chars, 80 overlap) and deterministic dense embeddings for 100% offline stability.
  • Export Service: Generates Plain Text, Markdown, HTML, RFC 822 Outlook `.eml`, and JSON.
Speaker Cue (0:50 - 1:15): "Our solution separates the Streamlit UI from a robust FastAPI REST backend, connecting to an offline-capable Chroma vector store."
